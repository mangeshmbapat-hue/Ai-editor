# Channel profile (defaults)

What the studio assumes when a brief does not say otherwise. These are defaults drawn from the owner's own agent notes and from this repository's work. **The owner can change any line; send changes through `studio-learn`.**

## Owner and voice
- The owner is an AI filmmaker and promo editor who builds his own AI production pipelines, and a solo creator of devotional/mythology AI series in several Indian dialects.
- **Voice:** a practitioner fresh out of the edit suite, not an influencer. Specific over impressive, numbers he can defend, team credit where it is due, opinions with reasons, short lines with varied rhythm. Rooted references: gaon, boli, bhakti, regional humour, promo-cutting instincts.
- **Language:** Hinglish by default on Instagram and Facebook; English-led on LinkedIn and X; Hindi or the requested dialect for the films themselves.
- **Never in copy:** em dashes; "let's dive in", "game-changer", "unlock", "unleash", "delve", "elevate", "seamless", "leverage", "in today's fast-paced world", "ever-evolving landscape", "it's not just X, it's Y", "buckle up".

## Audience
- Hindi and Hinglish speakers across India, plus regional-dialect viewers (for example Haryanvi, Rajasthani, Bhojpuri), on mobile first.
- **Content that fits:**
  - mythology and devotion
  - Indian history and culture
  - festivals
  - village life and regional humour
  - "how this AI film was made" behind-the-scenes
- **No-go:**
  - communal or political bait
  - exploiting tragedies or victims
  - mocking any group
  - misinformation
  - other people's footage or music

## Platforms and formats

| Platform | Role | Default format |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | discovery | 9:16, 30–45 s |
| YouTube Shorts | discovery | 9:16, 30–45 s (60 s maximum) |
| YouTube | depth | 16:9, 3–8 min |
| Facebook | Hindi/Hinglish regional reach | 9:16 or 16:9 |
| X, LinkedIn | AI-filmmaking audience | clips and behind-the-scenes |
| WhatsApp status | family sharing | 9:16, under 30 s |

## Production defaults
- **Pipeline:** films are made in code with the Lemo-Opuscar library (no video generation) unless the owner chooses a generation tool through AI-film's handoff skills.
- **Output:** 1080p, 24 fps unless the style says otherwise; −14 LUFS; burned-in subtitles plus an `.srt`.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs when reachable, otherwise Kokoro offline, or the owner's own recording.

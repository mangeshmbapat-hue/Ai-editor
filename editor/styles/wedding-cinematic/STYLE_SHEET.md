# Style sheet: Cinematic wedding film

`wedding-cinematic` · family **music** · known from: Indian wedding films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: weddings, family events, celebrations. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 1-8 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.5s · about 4 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

A vow or letter line over a slow-motion detail.

## Structure

Preparation, emotion, ceremony, celebration, vows to end.

## Cutting

Slow motion for emotion, real time for dance; vows as voice-over.

## Camera and framing

Gimbal, long lens candids, drone.

## Transitions

Dissolves, light leaks.

## Typography and captions

Elegant script names and date.

## Motion graphics

Monogram.

## Colour

Warm, soft, golden.

## Sound design

Emotional score, vows and speeches as voice-over, dhol for celebration.

## VFX

Light leaks, slow motion.

## Signature moves

- vows as VO
- slow-motion emotion
- golden grade
- dhol energy shift

## Avoid

- harsh contrast
- unflattering frames

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Edited as a film trailer with title cards

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation any, footage: broll, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style wedding-cinematic`)

- Grade preset `golden-hour`
- Transition `dissolve` every 3 cuts, 0.5s
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -12 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

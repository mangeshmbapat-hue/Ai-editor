# Style sheet: Retention maximalist

`retention-maximalist` · family **social** · known from: MrBeast videos · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: challenges, big reveals, high-energy entertainment. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 45 s-15 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.2s · about 8 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.6s

## Hook

Show the end state or the stakes in the first second, then promise the payoff.

## Structure

Stakes, escalation every 20-40 s, countdown or scoreboard, payoff, quick exit.

## Cutting

Something changes on screen every 1-2 s: angle, zoom, text, graphic or SFX.

## Camera and framing

Many angles, wide lenses, reaction close-ups.

## Transitions

Hard cuts, whip pans, zoom transitions on scene changes.

## Typography and captions

Big outlined numbers and stakes text; money and time counters.

## Motion graphics

Counters, timers, arrows, circle highlights, map pins.

## Colour

Bright, saturated, clean whites.

## Sound design

Constant music bed with risers, hits on reveals, cartoon SFX, music drops out for key lines.

## VFX

Zoom punches, screen shake on impacts, freeze-frame reactions.

## Signature moves

- stakes in second one
- counter on screen
- re-hook every 30 s
- reaction close-ups
- music drop before reveal

## Avoid

- any shot over 4 s without change
- explaining what is visible

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Countdown timer that becomes the transition
- Reveal shown first, then rewound

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion high, orientation any, footage: action, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style retention-maximalist`)

- Grade preset `bright-clean`
- Transition `zoomin` every 6 cuts, 0.25s
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.15
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.2s removed
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: 3 word(s) per caption, 6% of frame height, center, white, uppercase
- FX every N clips: shake /7

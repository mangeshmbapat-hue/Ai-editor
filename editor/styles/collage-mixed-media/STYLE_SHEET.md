# Style sheet: Collage mixed media

`collage-mixed-media` · family **experimental** · known from: collage animation and zine-style edits · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: art, culture, personal essays. Aspect any, duration 15 s-5 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.5s · about 6.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

A cut-out figure pasted onto an unexpected background.

## Structure

Layers added and torn away.

## Cutting

Stop-motion stutter at 8-12 fps on graphics.

## Camera and framing

Stills and cut-outs.

## Transitions

Paper tears, wipes.

## Typography and captions

Ransom-note mixed fonts, stamps.

## Motion graphics

Cut-out layers, tape, scribbles.

## Colour

Paper textures, halftones.

## Sound design

Paper rustles, scissors, playful score.

## VFX

Halftone, stop-motion frame rate.

## Signature moves

- cut-out layers
- stop-motion stutter
- paper SFX

## Avoid

- smooth digital motion

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Collage from old Indian matchbox and poster art

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion low, orientation any, footage: stills.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style collage-mixed-media`)

- Grade preset `faded-vintage`, grain 6
- Transition `wipeleft` every 3 cuts, 0.25s
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.08
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX on all clips: stop_motion

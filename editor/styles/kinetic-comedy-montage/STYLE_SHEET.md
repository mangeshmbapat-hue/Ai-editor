# Style sheet: Kinetic comedy montage

`kinetic-comedy-montage` · family **cinematic** · known from: Edgar Wright films (Hot Fuzz, Shaun of the Dead) · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: comedy, everyday routines made epic, prep sequences. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 0.7s · about 14 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.5s

## Hook

A mundane action shot like an action-film close-up.

## Structure

Rapid 'tool-up' montages bracketed by deadpan holds.

## Cutting

Ultra-short close-ups cut exactly on SFX; whip pans as transitions; the joke lands on a held wide.

## Camera and framing

Extreme close-ups of objects, whip pans, crash zooms.

## Transitions

Whip pans and match cuts between scenes.

## Typography and captions

Minimal.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Rich, punchy.

## Sound design

Every cut has a designed sound (door, click, swish); music synced to action.

## VFX

Whip blur, crash zoom.

## Signature moves

- SFX on every cut
- mundane made epic
- whip-pan transitions
- deadpan hold after montage

## Avoid

- silent cuts
- random shot order

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The montage plays backwards for the undo joke

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion high, orientation any, footage: action, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style kinetic-comedy-montage`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `smoothleft` every 8 cuts, 0.15s
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.25
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

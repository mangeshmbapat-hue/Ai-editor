# Style sheet: POV storytime

`pov-storytime` · family **social** · known from: POV micro-drama Reels · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: relatable scenarios, micro-drama, skits. Aspect 9:16, duration 15-45 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.8s · about 5.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.2s

## Hook

"POV:" text over the first frame sets the situation instantly.

## Structure

Set-up text, escalation, twist, loopable last beat.

## Cutting

Cut on reactions; tension held with one longer shot before the twist.

## Camera and framing

First-person or close single, handheld.

## Transitions

Hard cuts, smash cut to the twist.

## Typography and captions

POV text top-centre, native-platform look.

## Motion graphics

None; looks unedited on purpose.

## Colour

Natural phone look.

## Sound design

Trending-sound energy (use original or licensed audio), silence before the twist.

## VFX

None, or a zoom on the twist.

## Signature moves

- POV text
- native feel
- smash cut twist
- loop ending

## Avoid

- polished graphics that break the native feel

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The last frame matches the first so it loops

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation vertical, footage: talking, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style pov-storytime`)

- Grade preset `neutral`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: 5 word(s) per caption, 5% of frame height, top, white, boxed

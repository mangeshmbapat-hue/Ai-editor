# Style sheet: Parallel cross-cut build

`parallel-crosscut` · family **cinematic** · known from: Christopher Nolan films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: thrillers, deadlines, multiple storylines converging. Aspect 16:9, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.2s · about 4.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

A ticking deadline stated or shown.

## Structure

Two or three timelines cross-cut, shots shortening as they converge.

## Cutting

Cross-cutting with accelerating rhythm; sound bridges link timelines.

## Camera and framing

Wide anamorphic frames, practical locations.

## Transitions

Hard cuts, sound bridges (J-cuts).

## Typography and captions

Minimal place/time titles.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Cool, high contrast, natural.

## Sound design

Ticking pulse, rising tone, heavy low end.

## VFX

Letterbox for scale.

## Signature moves

- accelerating cross-cuts
- ticking pulse
- J-cut sound bridges

## Avoid

- losing the viewer in which timeline is which

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Each timeline has its own grade so the cross-cuts read instantly

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation horizontal, footage: action, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style parallel-crosscut`)

- Grade preset `cool-night`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -14 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX on all clips: letterbox

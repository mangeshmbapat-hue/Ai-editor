# Style sheet: Seamless travel transitions

`travel-transitions` · family **music** · known from: Sam Kolder travel films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: travel, adventure, brand films. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 30 s-4 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.2s · about 8 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

A whip or spin transition into a breathtaking wide.

## Structure

Journey montage, peak moment, calm ending.

## Cutting

Motion-matched transitions (whip, spin, zoom, mask through objects); speed ramps.

## Camera and framing

Gimbal moves, drones, whip pans planned for transitions.

## Transitions

Whip, spin, zoom-through, object masks.

## Typography and captions

Minimal location text.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Teal-orange, rich skies.

## Sound design

Energetic track, whooshes on every transition, ambience layers.

## VFX

Speed ramps, masked transitions.

## Signature moves

- seamless transitions
- speed ramps
- whooshes
- drones

## Avoid

- static tripod shots

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Every transition passes through a door or window of Indian architecture

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion high, orientation any, footage: broll, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style travel-transitions`)

- Grade preset `teal-orange`
- Transition `smoothleft` every 2 cuts, 0.25s
- Zoom: every 3 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.12
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -12 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

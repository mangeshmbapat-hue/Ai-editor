# Style sheet: Noir voice-over

`noir-voiceover` · family **cinematic** · known from: 1940s film noir · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: mystery, true crime, confessions. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.5s · about 2.8 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 3.0s

## Hook

A first-person line of fatalistic voice-over over a shadowy image.

## Structure

Flashback framed by narration.

## Cutting

Slow, deliberate; dissolves for time jumps.

## Camera and framing

Low key, venetian-blind shadows, Dutch angles.

## Transitions

Dissolves, fades to black.

## Typography and captions

Classic serif titles.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Black and white, crushed blacks.

## Sound design

Muted trumpet or piano, rain, city ambience.

## VFX

Grain, vignette.

## Signature moves

- fatalistic VO
- hard shadows
- B&W
- dissolves

## Avoid

- bright colour

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Only one object stays in colour (selective colour)

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion low, orientation any, footage: broll, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style noir-voiceover`)

- Grade preset `bw-contrast`, grain 8, vignette
- Transition `dissolve` every 3 cuts, 0.6s
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.08
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

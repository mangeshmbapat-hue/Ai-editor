# Style sheet: Crime caper snap

`crime-caper-snap` · family **cinematic** · known from: Guy Ritchie films (Snatch, Lock, Stock) · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: character intros, heists, swagger stories. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.1s · about 9 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.8s

## Hook

Freeze frame on a character with a name title.

## Structure

Parallel threads cross-cut until they collide.

## Cutting

Clip editing: speed ramps, freeze frames, rewinds, parallel editing.

## Camera and framing

Low angles, snap zooms, split screens.

## Transitions

Hard cuts, speed-ramp transitions.

## Typography and captions

Character name freeze-frame titles.

## Motion graphics

Polyscreen split frames.

## Colour

Desaturated, gritty, bleach-bypass.

## Sound design

Eclectic soundtrack, record-scratch rewinds, punchy hits.

## VFX

Freeze frames, speed ramps.

## Signature moves

- freeze-frame name titles
- speed ramps
- parallel threads
- rewinds

## Avoid

- clean bright looks

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Rewind effect to show the same moment from another character

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion high, orientation any, footage: action, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style crime-caper-snap`)

- Grade preset `bleach-bypass`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 3 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.2
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.2s removed
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- freeze first clip (s): 0.8

# Style sheet: Clean productivity explainer

`clean-productivity` · family **social** · known from: Ali Abdaal style breakdowns · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: education, productivity, calm explanation. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 45 s-15 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.2s · about 3 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

A relatable problem stated in one line, with the answer teased.

## Structure

Problem, numbered ideas with examples, recap.

## Cutting

Unhurried; B-roll of hands, desk, apps under voice.

## Camera and framing

Soft-lit medium shot, shallow depth of field, clean set.

## Transitions

Cuts and gentle dissolves.

## Typography and captions

Handwritten-style or rounded sans captions; idea number cards.

## Motion graphics

Screen recordings in rounded frames, highlight underline animations.

## Colour

Muted, grey-scale backgrounds with one accent colour that pops.

## Sound design

Lo-fi or acoustic bed, soft clicks on graphics.

## VFX

Selective colour pop on the key object.

## Signature moves

- one accent colour
- numbered idea cards
- rounded screen frames
- calm pace

## Avoid

- loud SFX
- clashing colours

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The accent colour tracks the idea count

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation any, footage: talking, screen, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style clean-productivity`)

- Grade preset `bright-clean`
- Transition `dissolve` every 5 cuts, 0.3s
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.4s removed
- Music at -22 dB
- Captions: 4 word(s) per caption, 5% of frame height, bottom, white, boxed

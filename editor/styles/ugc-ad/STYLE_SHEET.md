# Style sheet: UGC product ad

`ugc-ad` · family **social** · known from: direct-response UGC ads · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: products, apps, D2C brands. Aspect 9:16, duration 15-40 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.5s · about 6.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

Pain point or bold result in the first second, product visible by second 3.

## Structure

Hook, problem, product demo, proof, offer and call to action.

## Cutting

Fast talking-head jump cuts intercut with product close-ups.

## Camera and framing

Handheld selfie, macro product shots.

## Transitions

Hard cuts.

## Typography and captions

Native captions, benefit callouts.

## Motion graphics

Price tag, star rating, before/after labels, CTA button.

## Colour

Bright, true-to-product colour.

## Sound design

Upbeat bed, pop SFX on callouts.

## VFX

Zoom on product, split-screen comparison.

## Signature moves

- pain-point hook
- product by 3 s
- proof shot
- clear CTA

## Avoid

- claims without proof
- brand logo as the hook

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The product solves the problem in a single match cut

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion mid, orientation vertical, footage: talking, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style ugc-ad`)

- Grade preset `bright-clean`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.15
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.25s removed
- Music at -20 dB
- Captions: 3 word(s) per caption, 6% of frame height, center, white

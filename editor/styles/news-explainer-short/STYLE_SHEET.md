# Style sheet: News explainer short

`news-explainer-short` · family **social** · known from: Indian news-explainer Reels and Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: current affairs, Hindi explainers, 'what happened' stories. Aspect 9:16, duration 30-75 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.0s · about 5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

The surprising fact as text plus voice in the first second.

## Structure

Fact, background in three beats, why it matters, open question.

## Cutting

Archival and stock images change on every sentence.

## Camera and framing

Stills with push-ins, maps, presenter optional.

## Transitions

Cuts, quick zoom on maps.

## Typography and captions

Bold Hindi/English headline text top, captions centre.

## Motion graphics

Map pins, date stamps, source credits.

## Colour

Contrasty with a brand accent (red or yellow).

## Sound design

Tense pulse bed, whoosh on headline changes.

## VFX

Ken Burns on stills.

## Signature moves

- fact-first hook
- headline bar
- Ken Burns stills
- source credits on screen

## Avoid

- claims without on-screen sources

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Timeline scrubber at the bottom showing where in history each beat sits

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation vertical, footage: stills, broll, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style news-explainer-short`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.15
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.25s removed
- Music at -22 dB
- Captions: 3 word(s) per caption, 6% of frame height, center, yellow

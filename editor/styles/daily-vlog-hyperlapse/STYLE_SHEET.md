# Style sheet: Daily vlog with hyperlapses

`daily-vlog-hyperlapse` · family **social** · known from: Casey Neistat vlogs · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: day-in-the-life, travel days, city stories. Aspect 16:9, duration 5-12 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.0s · about 5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

Direct address with the day's mission, then a fast hyperlapse.

## Structure

Mission, journey montages, obstacle, resolution, sign-off.

## Cutting

Talking pieces cut tight, music montages between them.

## Camera and framing

Handheld selfie, tripod-on-the-ground shots, drones, time-lapse.

## Transitions

Hard cuts on beat; speed ramps into hyperlapses.

## Typography and captions

Handwritten notes, location titles.

## Motion graphics

Map line for travel.

## Colour

Contrasty, slightly cool city look.

## Sound design

Music-driven montages; natural sound kept in talk sections.

## VFX

Speed ramps, time-lapse.

## Signature moves

- mission statement
- hyperlapse montage
- on-camera walk-and-talk
- music-led transitions

## Avoid

- static long takes

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The whole day told as one continuous hyperlapse with voice-over

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion high, orientation horizontal, footage: broll, talking, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style daily-vlog-hyperlapse`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- montage speed: 2.0

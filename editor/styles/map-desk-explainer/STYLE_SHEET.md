# Style sheet: Map and desk explainer

`map-desk-explainer` · family **documentary** · known from: Johnny Harris videos · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: geography, geopolitics, travel investigations. Aspect 16:9, duration 5-20 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.5s · about 4 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.5s

## Hook

A map zoom from space to a single place with a question.

## Structure

Question, investigation on the desk and on location, answer.

## Cutting

Presenter, map zooms and paper-on-desk inserts intercut.

## Camera and framing

Overhead desk shots with hands, handheld on location.

## Transitions

Map zoom transitions.

## Typography and captions

Handwritten labels, typewriter text.

## Motion graphics

3D map flyovers, route lines, pins.

## Colour

Warm, textured, film look.

## Sound design

Textured ambient score, paper and pen foley.

## VFX

Grain, paper textures.

## Signature moves

- map zooms
- hands on desk
- handwritten labels
- route lines

## Avoid

- maps without labels

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The map is a hand-painted Indian scroll

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion mid, orientation horizontal, footage: talking, broll, stills.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style map-desk-explainer`)

- Grade preset `warm-film`, grain 5
- Transition `zoomin` every 6 cuts, 0.4s
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.15
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -22 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

# Style sheet: Kinetic typography

`kinetic-typography` · family **experimental** · known from: lyric videos and type-led promos · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: quotes, lyrics, manifestos, speeches. Aspect any, duration 10-90 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.0s · about 10 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.5s

## Hook

The first word slams in on the first beat.

## Structure

Text is the picture; every phrase has its own motion.

## Cutting

Text changes on syllables and beats.

## Camera and framing

Type on colour fields, occasional footage behind.

## Transitions

Type morphs, wipes.

## Typography and captions

Two typefaces maximum, size contrast for emphasis.

## Motion graphics

Built in Hyperframes: scale, slide, mask reveals synced to voice.

## Colour

Two-colour palette.

## Sound design

Voice or song drives the timing; soft whooshes.

## VFX

None.

## Signature moves

- text as image
- syllable timing
- size contrast

## Avoid

- more than two fonts
- text too fast to read

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Devanagari letters assemble from their strokes

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation any, footage: talking, screen.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style kinetic-typography`)

- Grade preset `neutral`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.25s removed
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: 1 word(s) per caption, 10% of frame height, center, white, uppercase

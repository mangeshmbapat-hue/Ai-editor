# Style sheet: Soft aesthetic vlog

`soft-aesthetic-vlog` · family **social** · known from: slow-living and 'that girl' vlogs · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: lifestyle, cooking, routines, calm content. Aspect 9:16 or 16:9, duration 20 s-10 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.8s · about 3.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.5s

## Hook

A beautiful detail shot (light, steam, hands) with ambient sound.

## Structure

Morning-to-night or process order, gentle text notes.

## Cutting

Cut on small actions, ASMR moments held.

## Camera and framing

Tripod and gentle handheld, close details, natural light.

## Transitions

Cuts and slow dissolves.

## Typography and captions

Small lowercase serif or handwritten notes.

## Motion graphics

None or tiny date stamp.

## Colour

Warm, soft contrast, film grain.

## Sound design

Lo-fi or acoustic bed, foley up front (pouring, pages).

## VFX

Grain, light leaks rarely.

## Signature moves

- detail shots
- foley forward
- lowercase notes
- warm grain

## Avoid

- loud SFX
- fast zooms

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Entire video in one colour family, changing with the time of day

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion low, orientation any, footage: broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style soft-aesthetic-vlog`)

- Grade preset `warm-film`, grain 6
- Transition `dissolve` every 4 cuts, 0.5s
- Zoom: every 3 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.06
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: 6 word(s) per caption, 4% of frame height, bottom, white

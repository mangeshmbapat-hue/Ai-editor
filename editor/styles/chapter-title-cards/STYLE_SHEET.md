# Style sheet: Chapter title cards

`chapter-title-cards` · family **cinematic** · known from: Quentin Tarantino films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: multi-part stories, non-linear narratives. Aspect 16:9, duration long.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 4.5s · about 2.2 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 3.0s

## Hook

A tense cold open dialogue, then the title.

## Structure

Named chapters, out of order; long dialogue scenes then sudden violence of pace.

## Cutting

Long held dialogue, then crash zooms and snap cuts at the turn.

## Camera and framing

Trunk shots, crash zooms, low angles.

## Transitions

Hard cuts, black title cards.

## Typography and captions

Bold chapter cards on black.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Warm, film-like.

## Sound design

Needle-drop era songs, silence in tension.

## VFX

Crash zoom.

## Signature moves

- chapter cards
- long tension holds
- crash zooms
- non-linear order

## Avoid

- constant fast cutting

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Chapter numbers counting down instead of up

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation horizontal, footage: talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style chapter-title-cards`)

- Grade preset `warm-film`, grain 5
- Transition `fadeblack` every 6 cuts, 0.5s
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

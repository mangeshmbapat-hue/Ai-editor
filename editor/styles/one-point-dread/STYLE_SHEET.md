# Style sheet: One-point dread

`one-point-dread` · family **cinematic** · known from: Stanley Kubrick films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: unease, power, institutions, horror. Aspect 16:9, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 5.5s · about 1.8 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 4.0s

## Hook

A long one-point corridor with a slow push in.

## Structure

Long held scenes, classical score against violence or calm.

## Cutting

Few cuts; slow zooms do the work.

## Camera and framing

One-point perspective, slow zooms, symmetrical corridors.

## Transitions

Hard cuts, title cards.

## Typography and captions

Clean sans title cards.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Cool, clinical or saturated reds.

## Sound design

Classical pieces, droning tones.

## VFX

Slow zoom.

## Signature moves

- one-point perspective
- slow zoom
- classical against image

## Avoid

- shaky camera
- fast cutting

## Unique twists (experiments)

- A Kubrick stare cut into a cooking video

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion low, orientation horizontal, footage: broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style one-point-dread`)

- Grade preset `cool-night`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.12
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

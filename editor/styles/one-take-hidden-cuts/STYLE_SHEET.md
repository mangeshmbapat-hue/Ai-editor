# Style sheet: One-take with hidden cuts

`one-take-hidden-cuts` · family **experimental** · known from: oner sequences in films and music videos · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: tours, reveals, immersive stories. Aspect any, duration 20 s-5 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 8.0s · about 1.2 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 6.0s

## Hook

The camera starts moving with intent in the first frame.

## Structure

One journey with reveals along the way.

## Cutting

Cuts hidden in whip pans, dark frames or objects passing the lens.

## Camera and framing

Gimbal or Steadicam, choreographed.

## Transitions

Hidden: whip, darkness, foreground wipe.

## Typography and captions

None or in-world text.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Consistent across the take.

## Sound design

Continuous ambience and music; no audio jumps.

## VFX

Masked stitches.

## Signature moves

- no visible cuts
- choreographed reveals
- continuous sound

## Avoid

- audio jumps at stitches

## Unique twists (experiments)

- A one-take walk through a crowded Indian market

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation any, footage: broll, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style one-take-hidden-cuts`)

- Grade preset `neutral`
- Transition `smoothleft` every 1 cuts, 0.3s
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

# Style sheet: Velocity edit

`velocity-edit` · family **music** · known from: CapCut velocity edits, anime AMVs · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: fan edits, characters, cars, dance. Aspect 9:16, duration 10-30 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.0s · about 10 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.5s

## Hook

A speed-ramped shot snapping to slow motion on the drop.

## Structure

Fast-slow-fast ramps on every phrase.

## Cutting

Speed curves within each clip: fast in, slow on the beat, fast out.

## Camera and framing

Any clip with clear motion.

## Transitions

Flash, zoom, shake.

## Typography and captions

Minimal or glowing text.

## Motion graphics

Glow overlays.

## Colour

High saturation or cool blue.

## Sound design

Phonk or remix with heavy bass.

## VFX

Speed ramps, shake, glow, flashes.

## Signature moves

- speed curves
- bass-drop slow motion
- shake and flash

## Avoid

- clips without motion

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Velocity edit of a classical dancer's mudras

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion high, orientation vertical, footage: action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style velocity-edit`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `zoomin` every 4 cuts, 0.2s
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.2
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -8 dB, beat-synced cuts
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX every N clips: shake /2, flash /4
- alternating speeds: [1.8, 0.6]

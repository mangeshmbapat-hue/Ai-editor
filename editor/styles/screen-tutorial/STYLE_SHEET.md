# Style sheet: Screen tutorial

`screen-tutorial` · family **social** · known from: software tutorial channels · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: apps, tools, how-to. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 30 s-10 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.5s · about 2.8 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

Show the finished result first, then how.

## Structure

Result, numbered steps, recap.

## Cutting

Cut out loading and typing; speed up repetitive actions.

## Camera and framing

Screen capture with face cam in a corner.

## Transitions

Cuts; slide for step changes.

## Typography and captions

Step labels, keyboard shortcut chips.

## Motion graphics

Zoom into the cursor area, highlight boxes, click ripples.

## Colour

Untouched screen colours.

## Sound design

Clear voice, soft click SFX.

## VFX

Auto-zoom on cursor regions.

## Signature moves

- result first
- zoom to cursor
- step labels
- dead time removed

## Avoid

- tiny unreadable UI on phones

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Speed-run version at the end in 10 seconds

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation horizontal, footage: screen.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style screen-tutorial`)

- Grade preset `neutral`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.35
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.5s removed
- Music at -28 dB
- Captions: 5 word(s) per caption, 4% of frame height, bottom, white, boxed

# Style sheet: Dreamlike surreal

`dreamlike-surreal` · family **experimental** · known from: surreal music videos and AI-video art · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: dreams, memories, AI-generated footage, art pieces. Aspect any, duration 15 s-3 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.5s · about 4 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

An impossible image held just long enough to register.

## Structure

Dream logic: images morph into each other.

## Cutting

Long dissolves and morphs, reverse motion, slow motion.

## Camera and framing

Floating moves, unusual perspectives.

## Transitions

Morphs, long dissolves, radial wipes.

## Typography and captions

Sparse, floating text.

## Motion graphics

Particles, soft light.

## Colour

Pastel or saturated dream palette, glow.

## Sound design

Reverb-heavy pads, reversed sounds, distant voices.

## VFX

Glow, reverse, slow motion, double exposure.

## Signature moves

- morph dissolves
- reverse motion
- glow
- reverb sound

## Avoid

- hard realistic cuts

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Every transition morphs through the colour of the next scene

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion mid, orientation any, footage: broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style dreamlike-surreal`)

- Grade preset `pastel`
- Transition `dissolve` every 1 cuts, 0.8s
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.08
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -14 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX on all clips: glow

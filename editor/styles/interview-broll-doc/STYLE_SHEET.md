# Style sheet: Interview and B-roll documentary

`interview-broll-doc` · family **documentary** · known from: brand and short documentaries · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: founder stories, profiles, testimonials. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 1-10 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.5s · about 2.8 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 3.0s

## Hook

An emotional line from the subject over an evocative B-roll shot.

## Structure

Who, struggle, turning point, now.

## Cutting

A-roll interview with B-roll covering every edit point; J- and L-cuts.

## Camera and framing

Two-camera interview, cinematic B-roll.

## Transitions

Cuts, J- and L-cuts.

## Typography and captions

Clean lower thirds.

## Motion graphics

Name and title lower third.

## Colour

Filmic, gentle contrast, matched skin.

## Sound design

Piano or ambient score rising at the turning point.

## VFX

None.

## Signature moves

- B-roll covers edits
- J/L cuts
- emotional score arc

## Avoid

- jump cuts left uncovered

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The subject's own home videos as B-roll

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation any, footage: talking, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style interview-broll-doc`)

- Grade preset `warm-film`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.5s removed
- Music at -20 dB
- Captions: 5 word(s) per caption, 4% of frame height, bottom, white
- B-roll insert every N cuts: 2

# Style sheet: Podcast clip

`podcast-clip` · family **social** · known from: podcast highlight Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: interviews, two-person conversations, debates. Aspect 9:16, duration 30-75 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.5s · about 4 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.5s

## Hook

Start mid-sentence on the most surprising line; context comes after.

## Structure

Hot line, context, the turn, the punchline; ends on a reaction or open question.

## Cutting

Cut to whoever is speaking; L-cuts let a reaction land under the other voice.

## Camera and framing

Stacked or alternating single shots reframed to 9:16; wide shot for laughs.

## Transitions

Hard cuts.

## Typography and captions

3-4 word captions, speaker colour-coded, lower-centre.

## Motion graphics

Name and title lower third once; occasional B-roll still with a slow push.

## Colour

Warm studio look, matched between cameras.

## Sound design

Voice only or a very low bed; room tone kept; no SFX on serious lines.

## VFX

Subtle punch-in on the punchline.

## Signature moves

- cold open mid-sentence
- speaker-coloured captions
- reaction shots
- ends on a hanging question

## Avoid

- cutting mid-word
- unmatched camera colour

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Split screen at the disagreement
- Freeze on the reaction with a one-word caption

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation horizontal, footage: talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style podcast-clip`)

- Grade preset `warm-film`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 4 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.1
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.45s removed
- Music at -28 dB
- Captions: 4 word(s) per caption, 6% of frame height, bottom, white, boxed, animated `karaoke` (yellow highlight)

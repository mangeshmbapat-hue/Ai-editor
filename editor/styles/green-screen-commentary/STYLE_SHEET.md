# Style sheet: Green-screen commentary

`green-screen-commentary` · family **social** · known from: news and screenshot commentary Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: reacting to headlines, posts, documents. Aspect 9:16, duration 20-60 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.5s · about 4 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

The headline or screenshot itself, with the creator pointing at it.

## Structure

Headline, what it means, what nobody says, opinion.

## Cutting

Background swaps on each new source; jump cuts on the speaker.

## Camera and framing

Creator keyed over the evidence, lower half of frame.

## Transitions

Cuts.

## Typography and captions

Highlight bars on screenshot text.

## Motion graphics

Zoom into quotes, highlighter swipes.

## Colour

Keep source colours; creator matched.

## Sound design

Voice only or low bed.

## VFX

Keyed overlay.

## Signature moves

- evidence behind the speaker
- highlighter swipes
- source changes on beats

## Avoid

- unreadable screenshots

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The speaker steps 'into' the screenshot with a zoom

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation vertical, footage: talking, stills.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style green-screen-commentary`)

- Grade preset `neutral`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.12
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.25s removed
- Music at -26 dB
- Captions: 3 word(s) per caption, 6% of frame height, center, white, boxed, animated `highlight` (yellow highlight)

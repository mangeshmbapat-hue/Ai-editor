# Style sheet: Fashion strobe edit

`fashion-strobe` · family **music** · known from: fashion films and lookbooks · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: fashion, beauty, lookbooks. Aspect 9:16, duration 10-40 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 0.6s · about 16 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.4s

## Hook

A flash-frame burst of looks.

## Structure

Look after look on the beat, final hero pose freeze.

## Cutting

Strobe cuts, repeated frames, freezes on poses.

## Camera and framing

Flash photography feel, studio backdrops.

## Transitions

Flash frames, hard cuts.

## Typography and captions

Editorial serif, large spaced uppercase.

## Motion graphics

Magazine-style layout frames.

## Colour

Clean high-key or high-contrast editorial.

## Sound design

House or techno, shutter SFX.

## VFX

Strobe flashes, freeze frames, RGB split.

## Signature moves

- strobe cuts
- pose freezes
- editorial type
- shutter SFX

## Avoid

- slow pacing

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Every look appears on a different Indian textile backdrop

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion mid, orientation vertical, footage: broll, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style fashion-strobe`)

- Grade preset `bright-clean`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.15
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -10 dB, beat-synced cuts
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX every N clips: flash /3
- freeze last clip (s): 0.8

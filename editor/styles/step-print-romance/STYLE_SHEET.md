# Style sheet: Step-printed romance

`step-print-romance` · family **cinematic** · known from: Wong Kar-wai films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: longing, memory, city nights, romance. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.8s · about 3.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

Smeared neon motion with an intimate voice-over line.

## Structure

Fragments of memory, repeated motifs, voice-over diary.

## Cutting

Elliptical; repeated slow-motion motifs; time stamps.

## Camera and framing

Handheld, through glass and doorways, step-printed motion.

## Transitions

Cuts, occasional dissolves.

## Typography and captions

Clock times and dates as text.

## Motion graphics

Time stamps.

## Colour

Saturated neon greens and reds, deep shadows.

## Sound design

A recurring waltz-like theme, rain, city hum.

## VFX

Step-print smear (frame blending at low frame rates), slow motion.

## Signature moves

- smeared motion
- neon palette
- repeated music motif
- time stamps

## Avoid

- clean static compositions

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Mumbai local train at night as the neon city

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion mid, orientation any, footage: broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style step-print-romance`)

- Grade preset `neon-night`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -14 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX on all clips: step_print

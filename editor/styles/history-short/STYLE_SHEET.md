# Style sheet: History short

`history-short` · family **documentary** · known from: Indian history and mythology Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: history, mythology, forgotten stories in Hindi. Aspect 9:16, duration 30-60 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.0s · about 5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

"Ye kahani kisi ne nahi batayi..." over a striking painting or still.

## Structure

Mystery, the story in three beats, twist, loop line.

## Cutting

Stills and paintings with push-ins, cut on every sentence.

## Camera and framing

Rostrum moves on paintings, maps.

## Transitions

Cuts, dissolves for time jumps.

## Typography and captions

Bold Hindi captions, date stamps.

## Motion graphics

Maps, era labels.

## Colour

Warm, aged, rich contrast.

## Sound design

Indian classical instruments, deep narration, drum hits on reveals.

## VFX

Grain, light dust.

## Signature moves

- mystery hook
- push-in on paintings
- Hindi captions
- era labels

## Avoid

- unlabelled myth vs history

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The painting comes alive with subtle parallax

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation vertical, footage: stills, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style history-short`)

- Grade preset `warm-film`, grain 6
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.14
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: 3 word(s) per caption, 6% of frame height, center, white, animated `highlight` (yellow highlight)

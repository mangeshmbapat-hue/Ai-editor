# Style sheet: Before/after transformation

`transformation-reveal` · family **social** · known from: glow-up and makeover Reels · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: makeovers, renovation, fitness, cleaning, art process. Aspect 9:16, duration 10-30 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 0.9s · about 10 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.5s

## Hook

The 'before' with a promise, or a flash of the 'after'.

## Structure

Before, fast process montage, held beat, reveal on the drop.

## Cutting

Accelerating process cuts, silence or riser, reveal exactly on the beat drop.

## Camera and framing

Locked angle so before and after match.

## Transitions

Match cut or flash at the reveal.

## Typography and captions

'Before'/'After' labels, day counters.

## Motion graphics

Progress counter.

## Colour

Before slightly flat, after graded richer.

## Sound design

Build-up track, drop at reveal, whoosh into it.

## VFX

White flash on reveal, speed ramp.

## Signature moves

- locked matching angle
- accelerating montage
- drop on reveal
- flash

## Avoid

- changing the angle between before and after

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Wipe that reveals the after inside the before frame

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion mid, orientation vertical, footage: broll, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style transformation-reveal`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -14 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX on the last clip: ['flash']
- montage speed: 2.0

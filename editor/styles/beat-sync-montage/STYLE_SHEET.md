# Style sheet: Beat-sync montage

`beat-sync-montage` · family **music** · known from: music-driven montage edits · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: events, recaps, highlights, travel. Aspect any, duration 15-90 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 0.8s · about 12 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.5s

## Hook

The best shot on the first beat.

## Structure

Intro bars slow, verse medium, chorus fast, outro slow.

## Cutting

Every cut on a beat; double-time on the chorus; motion matched across cuts.

## Camera and framing

Anything with movement.

## Transitions

Cuts on beat, flash on downbeats.

## Typography and captions

Minimal.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Unified grade across all clips.

## Sound design

One track carries everything; natural sound only for accents.

## VFX

Speed ramps, flashes on drops.

## Signature moves

- cut on every beat
- chorus double-time
- motion continuity

## Avoid

- off-beat cuts

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Cuts on the lyric words instead of the beat

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion high, orientation any, footage: action, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style beat-sync-montage`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -10 dB, beat-synced cuts
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX every N clips: flash /8

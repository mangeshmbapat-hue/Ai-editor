# Style sheet: Video essay

`video-essay` · family **documentary** · known from: Every Frame a Painting video essays · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: film analysis, criticism, ideas. Aspect 16:9, duration 6-20 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 4.0s · about 2.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 3.0s

## Hook

A clip that perfectly shows the thesis.

## Structure

Thesis, examples, counter-example, conclusion.

## Cutting

Clips cut to the narration's argument, side-by-side comparisons.

## Camera and framing

Clips and stills.

## Transitions

Cuts.

## Typography and captions

Clean sans labels with film titles and years.

## Motion graphics

Side-by-side frames, freeze and annotate.

## Colour

Untouched source.

## Sound design

Narration, source audio for examples, minimal music.

## VFX

Freeze frames.

## Signature moves

- thesis-led
- side-by-side
- freeze-and-annotate
- titles and years

## Avoid

- clips without a point

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The essay is edited in the style it analyses

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion any, orientation horizontal, footage: broll, stills.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style video-essay`)

- Grade preset `neutral`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.4s removed
- Music at -26 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

# Style sheet: Retro VHS camcorder

`retro-vhs` · family **experimental** · known from: 90s home videos and nostalgia edits · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: nostalgia, throwbacks, family memories. Aspect 4:3 or 9:16, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.5s · about 4 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

A camcorder REC and date stamp.

## Structure

Home-movie fragments, memory voice-over.

## Cutting

Rough in-camera style cuts, tape glitches between scenes.

## Camera and framing

Handheld, zoom rocker moves.

## Transitions

Tape glitch, cut to blue.

## Typography and captions

VCR mono font, date stamp bottom right.

## Motion graphics

REC dot, timecode.

## Colour

Bleeding chroma, soft, lifted blacks.

## Sound design

Tape hiss, wow and flutter, home-video audio.

## VFX

Scanlines, chroma shift, noise.

## Signature moves

- date stamp
- chroma bleed
- tape glitch
- handheld zooms

## Avoid

- crisp 4K look

## Unique twists (experiments)

- A modern event filmed like 1996

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation any, footage: broll, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style retro-vhs`)

- Grade preset `vhs-retro`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 3 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.1
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX on all clips: vhs

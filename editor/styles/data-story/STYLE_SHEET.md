# Style sheet: Data story

`data-story` · family **documentary** · known from: data-journalism explainers · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: statistics, economics, comparisons. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 30 s-8 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.8s · about 3.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.5s

## Hook

One shocking number counting up.

## Structure

Number, context, comparison, what changes.

## Cutting

Chart builds paced to the voice; cutaways to real footage.

## Camera and framing

Graphics-led.

## Transitions

Cuts, morphs between charts.

## Typography and captions

Big numerals, clean sans.

## Motion graphics

Animated bar, line and map charts, counters.

## Colour

Two-colour data palette on neutral.

## Sound design

Light electronic bed, tick SFX on counters.

## VFX

None.

## Signature moves

- counting numbers
- charts built on the voice
- two-colour palette

## Avoid

- charts without units or sources

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The chart bars are real objects (stacked rotis, coins)

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation any, footage: stills, broll, screen.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style data-story`)

- Grade preset `bright-clean`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.1
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -22 dB
- Captions: 4 word(s) per caption, 5% of frame height, bottom, white, boxed

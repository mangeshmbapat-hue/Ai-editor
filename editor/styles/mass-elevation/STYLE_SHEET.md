# Style sheet: South Indian mass elevation

`mass-elevation` · family **cinematic** · known from: Telugu, Tamil and Kannada mass films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: power moments, fan edits, sports heroes. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 15-60 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.0s · about 9 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.8s

## Hook

Silence, then a single impact sound and a low-angle shot.

## Structure

Threat, hero arrives, elevation shots, impact, title.

## Cutting

Ramp-in to slow-motion, cuts on every BGM accent, repeated hero angle twice.

## Camera and framing

Extreme low angles, rotating shots, dust and sparks.

## Transitions

Flash cuts, smash cuts.

## Typography and captions

Massive metallic title cards.

## Motion graphics

Fire, sparks, smoke overlays.

## Colour

High contrast, orange-teal or monochrome with red accents.

## Sound design

Thunderous percussion, choir chants, sub-bass hits, sudden silence.

## VFX

Shake, speed ramps, flash frames.

## Signature moves

- silence then impact
- low-angle elevation
- repeated hero shot
- percussion hits

## Avoid

- weak or thin music

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Mass treatment for a grandmother making chai

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion high, orientation any, footage: action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style mass-elevation`)

- Grade preset `moody-dark`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.2
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -10 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX every N clips: shake /3, flash /4
- last clip speed: 0.5

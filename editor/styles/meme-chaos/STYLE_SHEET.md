# Style sheet: Meme chaos edit

`meme-chaos` · family **social** · known from: meme and shitpost Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: comedy, gaming, absurd humour. Aspect 9:16, duration 10-40 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 0.9s · about 11 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.5s

## Hook

Absurd image or sound in the first half-second.

## Structure

Set-up, escalating absurdity, freeze-frame punchline.

## Cutting

Snap zooms, freeze frames, deliberate bad cuts for comedy.

## Camera and framing

Anything; crash zooms on faces.

## Transitions

Hard cuts, no polish.

## Typography and captions

Impact-style meme text, captions with emoji.

## Motion graphics

Stickers, arrows, red circles.

## Colour

Over-saturated, deep-fried optional.

## Sound design

Boom, record-scratch, bass-boosted hits, sudden silence.

## VFX

Crash zoom, shake, freeze frame, RGB split.

## Signature moves

- crash zoom
- freeze frame
- boom SFX
- sticker arrows

## Avoid

- subtlety

## Unique twists (experiments)

- A serious documentary narrator over the chaos

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion any, orientation vertical, footage: action, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style meme-chaos`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.35
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.2s removed
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: 2 word(s) per caption, 8% of frame height, center, white, uppercase
- FX every N clips: shake /3, rgb_split /5
- freeze last clip (s): 0.6

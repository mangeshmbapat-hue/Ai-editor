# Style sheet: Glitch and datamosh

`glitch-datamosh` · family **experimental** · known from: glitch-art music videos · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: tech, gaming, music, edgy promos. Aspect any, duration 10-60 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.1s · about 9 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.6s

## Hook

A clean image that glitches apart.

## Structure

Order breaking into chaos and back.

## Cutting

Glitch hits on beats, stutter repeats.

## Camera and framing

Any.

## Transitions

Pixelize, RGB split cuts.

## Typography and captions

Mono or pixel fonts.

## Motion graphics

Scanlines, UI fragments.

## Colour

Neon cyan and magenta.

## Sound design

Bit-crushed glitch SFX, electronic music.

## VFX

RGB split, pixel sorting, stutter.

## Signature moves

- RGB split
- pixelize transitions
- stutter cuts

## Avoid

- over-use that hides the subject

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Glitches reveal a hidden second image underneath

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation any, footage: action, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style glitch-datamosh`)

- Grade preset `neon-night`
- Transition `pixelize` every 3 cuts, 0.3s
- Zoom: every 3 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.1
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.2s removed
- Music at -12 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX every N clips: rgb_split /2

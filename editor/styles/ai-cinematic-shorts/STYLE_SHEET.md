# Style sheet: AI cinematic short

`ai-cinematic-shorts` · family **experimental** · known from: AI-generated short films on social platforms · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: AI-generated clips, concept trailers, story shorts. Aspect 9:16 or 16:9, duration 20-90 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.2s · about 4.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.5s

## Hook

The most impossible generated shot first, with a voice line.

## Structure

Trailer-like three beats; each generated clip used for its best 1-3 s only.

## Cutting

Cut before AI artefacts appear; hide morphing with cuts on motion; consistent character shots grouped.

## Camera and framing

Generated camera moves; prefer clips with one clear move.

## Transitions

Cuts, flash frames, dips to black between beats.

## Typography and captions

Cinematic title cards.

## Motion graphics

Letterbox, title cards.

## Colour

One unified grade to glue different generations together.

## Sound design

Designed soundscape carries continuity across mismatched clips: ambience, risers, hits, voice-over.

## VFX

Grain and letterbox unify; slow motion hides jitter.

## Signature moves

- best 1-3 s of each clip
- unified grade and grain
- sound carries continuity
- letterbox

## Avoid

- showing morphing hands or faces
- mixing clips with different looks ungraded

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The AI artefacts are used on purpose as a 'memory decaying' effect

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion mid, orientation any, footage: broll, action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style ai-cinematic-shorts`)

- Grade preset `teal-orange`, grain 5
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -14 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX on all clips: letterbox
- max clip length (s): 3.0

# Style sheet: Bollywood masala hero entry

`masala-hero-entry` · family **cinematic** · known from: Hindi commercial cinema · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: hero or product entries, celebrations, dramatic moments. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 15-90 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.3s · about 7.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

Feet, hands or silhouette first; the face held back.

## Structure

Build-up details, slow-motion reveal, punch dialogue, celebration.

## Cutting

Detail inserts accelerating to a slow-motion hero shot; cut on BGM hits.

## Camera and framing

Low angles, slow-motion, wind and dust, rotating dolly.

## Transitions

Whip cuts, white flashes on hits.

## Typography and captions

Big stylised title in Hindi and English.

## Motion graphics

Sparks, flares as overlays.

## Colour

Warm golden or teal-orange, rich contrast.

## Sound design

Heavy BGM with dhol or brass hits, whooshes, silence before the punch line.

## VFX

Speed ramps, flash frames, shake on hits.

## Signature moves

- face held back
- slow-mo reveal
- BGM hits
- punch-line silence

## Avoid

- revealing the hero too early

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The hero entry used for an everyday object, played straight

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion high, orientation any, footage: action, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style masala-hero-entry`)

- Grade preset `teal-orange`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 3 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.15
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -12 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX every N clips: flash /5, shake /6
- last clip speed: 0.5

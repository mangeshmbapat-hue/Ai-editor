# Style sheet: True-crime series

`true-crime-series` · family **documentary** · known from: streaming true-crime documentaries · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: crime, scandals, investigations. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.5s · about 2.8 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

The most chilling interview line over an evidence image.

## Structure

Cold open, timeline, interviews, reveal, cliffhanger.

## Cutting

Interviews intercut with slow evidence shots and reenactments.

## Camera and framing

Off-centre interview framing, slow sliders on evidence.

## Transitions

Cuts, dip to black at reveals.

## Typography and captions

Typewriter dates and case details.

## Motion graphics

Case boards, redacted documents.

## Colour

Desaturated, cold.

## Sound design

Ticking pulses, low drones, silence before revelations.

## VFX

Slow push, grain.

## Signature moves

- chilling cold open
- evidence close-ups
- typewriter dates
- cliffhanger

## Avoid

- sensationalising victims

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The case board builds itself across the episode

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation any, footage: talking, broll, stills.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style true-crime-series`)

- Grade preset `desat-doc`, vignette
- Transition `fadeblack` every 6 cuts, 0.4s
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.08
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.4s removed
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: 5 word(s) per caption, 4% of frame height, bottom, white

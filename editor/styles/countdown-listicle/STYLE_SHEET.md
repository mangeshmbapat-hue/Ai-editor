# Style sheet: Countdown listicle

`countdown-listicle` · family **social** · known from: top-5 and top-10 Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: rankings, facts, recommendations. Aspect 9:16 or 16:9, duration 30 s-12 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 2.2s · about 4.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

Tease number one before starting at five.

## Structure

Tease, 5 to 1 with a card each, number one held longest.

## Cutting

Each item: card, two or three supporting shots, verdict.

## Camera and framing

Mixed B-roll and talking.

## Transitions

Slide or whip between items.

## Typography and captions

Huge numerals, item titles.

## Motion graphics

Number cards, rank ladder.

## Colour

Consistent brand palette per item card.

## Sound design

Sting on each number, music lifts toward number one.

## VFX

None.

## Signature moves

- tease number one
- number cards
- sting per item

## Avoid

- equal time for every item

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The ranking reverses at the end

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation any, footage: broll, talking, stills.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style countdown-listicle`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `slideleft` every 3 cuts, 0.3s
- Zoom: every 3 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.1
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.3s removed
- Music at -20 dB
- Captions: 3 word(s) per caption, 6% of frame height, center, white, uppercase, animated `pop` (yellow highlight)

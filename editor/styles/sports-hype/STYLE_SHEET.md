# Style sheet: Sports hype

`sports-hype` · family **music** · known from: sports highlight and hype edits · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: sports, fitness, competitions. Aspect any, duration 15-60 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 0.9s · about 11 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 0.5s

## Hook

The biggest impact moment, frozen.

## Structure

Build, hits, the winning moment, title.

## Cutting

Speed ramps around impacts, cuts on bass hits.

## Camera and framing

Telephoto, slow motion, crowd.

## Transitions

Flashes, shake cuts.

## Typography and captions

Heavy condensed titles, stats.

## Motion graphics

Stat counters, player names.

## Colour

High contrast, crushed blacks.

## Sound design

Hard trap or orchestral hybrid, crowd roars, impact hits.

## VFX

Shake, flash, speed ramp, motion blur.

## Signature moves

- speed ramps on impact
- bass-hit cuts
- stat graphics
- crowd roar

## Avoid

- slow sections without purpose

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Gully cricket shot like a world-cup final

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion high, orientation any, footage: action.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style sports-hype`)

- Grade preset `moody-dark`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.2
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -10 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default
- FX every N clips: shake /2, flash /4
- last clip speed: 0.5

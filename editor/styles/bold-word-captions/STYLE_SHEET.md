# Style sheet: Bold word-by-word captions

`bold-word-captions` · family **social** · known from: Alex Hormozi Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: talking-head advice, business, motivation, one strong speaker. Aspect 9:16, duration 20-60 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.6s · about 6 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

The boldest claim as the first spoken words, on a tight punch-in, caption already on screen.

## Structure

Claim, proof or story, one-line takeaway; no intro, no outro.

## Cutting

Jump cuts remove every breath and pause over 0.25 s; punch-in alternates every 2 cuts.

## Camera and framing

Single locked medium shot; digital punch-ins 110-125 % fake a second angle.

## Transitions

Hard cuts only.

## Typography and captions

1-3 words per caption, heavy sans (800-900 weight), uppercase, white with the key word in yellow or green, centre frame, pop-in scale 90-105 %.

## Motion graphics

Occasional icon or emoji pop beside the key word; progress bar optional.

## Colour

Clean, contrasty, slightly saturated skin; dark background helps captions read.

## Sound design

Voice-forward, light bass bed at -22 dB, whoosh or click on punch-ins.

## VFX

None beyond punch-ins.

## Signature moves

- captions on every word
- zero dead air
- punch-in rhythm
- key word coloured
- claim in the first second

## Avoid

- slow intros
- captions in the bottom 20 % (platform UI)
- more than 3 words per caption

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Captions change colour with the speaker's emotion
- Key numbers counted up as motion graphics

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation any, footage: talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style bold-word-captions`)

- Grade preset `punchy-social`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.18
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.25s removed
- Music at -24 dB
- Captions: 2 word(s) per caption, 8% of frame height, center, white, uppercase, animated `highlight` (yellow highlight)

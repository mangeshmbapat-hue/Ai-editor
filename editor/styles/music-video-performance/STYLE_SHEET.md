# Style sheet: Music video performance

`music-video-performance` · family **music** · known from: performance music videos · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: songs, covers, performances. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration song length.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.5s · about 6.5 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.5s

## Hook

The artist's face on the first lyric.

## Structure

Performance intercut with narrative, lip-sync locked.

## Cutting

Cuts on bars and lyric phrases; multi-angle performance.

## Camera and framing

Multi-cam performance, stylised lighting.

## Transitions

Cuts, flashes.

## Typography and captions

Lyrics optional.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Bold stylised grade.

## Sound design

The song only.

## VFX

Flashes, strobe, speed changes (shoot fast, play slow).

## Signature moves

- lip-sync locked
- cuts on phrases
- performance and narrative intercut

## Avoid

- losing sync

## Unique twists (experiments)

- One continuous take for the bridge

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion mid, orientation any, footage: action, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style music-video-performance`)

- Grade preset `neon-night`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 4 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.1
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -8 dB, beat-synced cuts
- Captions: no burned captions by default

# Style sheet: Archival documentary (Ken Burns effect)

`archival-documentary` · family **documentary** · known from: Ken Burns documentaries · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: history, biographies, heritage. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 5.0s · about 2 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 4.0s

## Hook

A face from an old photograph with a quote read aloud.

## Structure

Chronological chapters, letters read by voices, interviews.

## Cutting

Slow pans and zooms on stills; long dissolves.

## Camera and framing

Rostrum moves on photographs.

## Transitions

Slow dissolves.

## Typography and captions

Classic serif lower thirds and dates.

## Motion graphics

Pan and zoom on stills.

## Colour

Sepia or neutral archival.

## Sound design

Period music, quiet ambience, read quotes.

## VFX

Ken Burns pans.

## Signature moves

- slow photo moves
- read quotes
- period music
- dissolves

## Avoid

- fast cutting

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Pan reveals a detail that changes the story

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion low, orientation any, footage: stills.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style archival-documentary`)

- Grade preset `faded-vintage`
- Transition `dissolve` every 1 cuts, 0.8s
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.12
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -18 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

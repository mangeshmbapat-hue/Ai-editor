# Style sheet: Symmetry storybook

`symmetry-storybook` · family **cinematic** · known from: Wes Anderson films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: whimsical stories, character introductions, travel postcards. Aspect 16:9 or 4:3, duration any.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.0s · about 3.3 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.5s

## Hook

A perfectly centred tableau with a title card.

## Structure

Chapters with title cards, deadpan narration.

## Cutting

Metric cuts on the music meter; symmetrical shot-reverse-shot; whip pans between centred frames.

## Camera and framing

Centred, flat, one-point compositions; lateral dollies; overhead inserts.

## Transitions

Whip pans, hard cuts.

## Typography and captions

Centred serif or Futura-style title cards.

## Motion graphics

Diagram inserts, labelled maps.

## Colour

Pastel palette, often two dominant colours.

## Sound design

Baroque or folk score, deadpan voice-over.

## VFX

None; theatrical miniatures feel.

## Signature moves

- dead-centre framing
- pastel palette
- chapter cards
- lateral moves

## Avoid

- handheld
- off-centre subjects

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Indian miniature-painting palette in a symmetry frame

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion low, orientation horizontal, footage: broll, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style symmetry-storybook`)

- Grade preset `pastel`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: 6 word(s) per caption, 4% of frame height, bottom, white

# Style sheet: Poetic nature elegy

`poetic-nature` · family **cinematic** · known from: Terrence Malick films · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: memories, nature, spiritual or philosophical pieces. Aspect 16:9, duration long.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.2s · about 3 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 3.0s

## Hook

Golden light through grass with a whispered question.

## Structure

Associative, not linear; whispered voice-over questions.

## Cutting

Elliptical cuts mid-action; never lingers on dialogue.

## Camera and framing

Moving handheld or Steadicam, magic hour, wide lenses, sky.

## Transitions

Cuts mid-motion.

## Typography and captions

None.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Natural golden-hour warmth.

## Sound design

Classical and choral, wind, whispered voice.

## VFX

None.

## Signature moves

- magic hour
- whispered VO
- elliptical cuts
- moving camera

## Avoid

- graphics
- text

## Unique twists (experiments)

- Indian village dawn edited as a prayer

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion mid, orientation horizontal, footage: broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style poetic-nature`)

- Grade preset `golden-hour`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: none
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -14 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

# Style sheet: Horror dread and jump

`horror-dread` · family **cinematic** · known from: modern horror films and creepy Shorts · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: horror stories, mysteries, urban legends. Aspect 9:16 or 16:9, duration 20 s-10 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.0s · about 3.3 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 3.0s

## Hook

Something slightly wrong in a calm frame.

## Structure

Normal, wrongness grows, long hold, scare, aftermath.

## Cutting

Uncomfortably long holds, then a jump cut on the scare.

## Camera and framing

Negative space, slow push, doorways.

## Transitions

Cuts, fades to black.

## Typography and captions

Minimal, small text.

## Motion graphics

None.

## Colour

Cold, dark, desaturated.

## Sound design

Low drones, silence before the scare, sharp stinger.

## VFX

Subtle shake on scare.

## Signature moves

- negative space
- long hold
- silence before stinger

## Avoid

- music that telegraphs the scare

## Unique twists (experiments)

- No scare at all: the tension never releases

## Footage it fits

Speech any, motion low, orientation any, footage: broll, talking.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style horror-dread`)

- Grade preset `cool-night`, vignette
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.08
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -16 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

# Style sheet: Moody cinematic B-roll

`cinematic-broll` · family **music** · known from: Peter McKinnon style B-roll · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: products, coffee, gear, intros. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 15-60 s.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 1.6s · about 6 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 1.0s

## Hook

A macro detail with sound (pour, click, steam).

## Structure

Detail build-up, the reveal shot, end card.

## Cutting

Cuts on foley sounds; parallax slider shots.

## Camera and framing

Macro, slider, shallow depth, practical lights.

## Transitions

Cuts, light-leak flashes.

## Typography and captions

Thin uppercase spaced titles.

## Motion graphics

Minimal title.

## Colour

Moody, crushed blacks, warm highlights.

## Sound design

Foley-forward, punchy beat.

## VFX

Light leaks, grain.

## Signature moves

- macro details
- foley-led cuts
- moody grade
- light leaks

## Avoid

- flat lighting

## Unique twists (experiments)

- One shot is a deliberate fake-out reveal

## Footage it fits

Speech low, motion low, orientation any, footage: broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style cinematic-broll`)

- Grade preset `moody-dark`, grain 4
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 2 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.08
- Silence cut: pauses kept
- Music at -14 dB
- Captions: no burned captions by default

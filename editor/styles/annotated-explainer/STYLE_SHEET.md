# Style sheet: Annotated explainer

`annotated-explainer` · family **documentary** · known from: Vox explainers · status **catalog (estimated)**

Best for: policy, history, why-questions. Aspect 16:9 or 9:16, duration 45 s-15 min.

> Pace numbers are estimated starting targets, not measured. Run `/mann-style-lab` on a reference the owner shares to replace them with measured ones.

## Pace (estimated)

- Average shot 3.0s · about 3.3 cuts per 10 s · first cut by 2.0s

## Hook

A surprising chart or archival image with the question.

## Structure

Question, layers of evidence, the answer, what it means.

## Cutting

Archival footage and documents animated in parallax.

## Camera and framing

Archival, documents, interviews.

## Transitions

Cuts, push-in through documents.

## Typography and captions

Clean sans, highlighter annotations on documents.

## Motion graphics

Paper texture, highlighter swipes, charts, maps.

## Colour

Desaturated archival with brand accent colour.

## Sound design

Ambient electronic bed, paper SFX.

## VFX

Parallax on stills.

## Signature moves

- highlighted documents
- paper texture
- charts
- question-led

## Avoid

- unsourced claims

## Unique twists (experiments)

- The annotations are hand-drawn in Devanagari

## Footage it fits

Speech high, motion low, orientation any, footage: stills, broll.

## Engine defaults (`autocut --style annotated-explainer`)

- Grade preset `desat-doc`
- Transition `cut`
- Zoom: every 1 clip(s), 1.0 -> 1.1
- Silence cut: gaps over 0.35s removed
- Music at -22 dB
- Captions: 5 word(s) per caption, 4% of frame height, bottom, white, boxed

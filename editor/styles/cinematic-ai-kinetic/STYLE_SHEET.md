## Hook and structure

- 0.0–0.8 s: the number is the hook. "TWENTY" in bold green 3D letters drops in with motion blur, then "BILLION" slams next to it while the background flips white → black (0.5 s) → green (0.9 s). VO "$20 billion" 0.00–0.32.
- 0.8–1.7 s: the object is the number: a green envelope stuffed with dollars falls in rotated, the "BILLION" text tilts off its edge on a ribbon.
- 1.4–2.0 s: the background turns light; the envelope lands on a small wooden stool, a grassy landscape and clouds grow in behind ("on the table").
- 2.1–3.0 s: the colour drains to B&W; "HE SAID" in red bold condensed sans, then a huge "NO" fades up under it ("He said no" 2.02–2.32).
- 3.1 s: a hard cut to a deep-red field with a faint building (Intel's offer): B&W hand cutouts float in, one holding the money, then a second holding the Nvidia logo, joined by thin red wireframe hexagons.
- 4.7–5.3 s: "2008" builds digit by digit, each digit rising out of a blur ("in 2008" 4.18–4.52; lag ~0.5 s).
- 5.9–6.4 s: Jensen Huang rises from the bottom, out of blur, in front of the "2008", and covers it.
- 9.0 s: a single-frame negative/invert flash, then B&W with a red glow on his glasses ("he could see something nobody else…").

## Cutting

Very fast: 10 cuts in 11.75 s, mostly 0.1–0.4 s flashes in the first 1.3 s, then holds of 2–6 s once the character is on screen. Cuts land on the number words ("twenty", "billion", "no", "2008").

## Camera and framing

Centred, symmetrical 9:16. Objects fall and rotate toward the camera; heavy motion blur and rack-focus blur are used as the reveal. Slow push on the held portrait.

## Transitions

Background colour flips (white → black → green → light) on the beat; blur-to-sharp reveals; a hard cut to the red field; a one-frame invert flash as a hit.

## Typography and captions

Heavy condensed or wide display sans, all caps ("TWENTY BILLION", "HE SAID NO"); numbers in rounded white sans ("2008"). Text colour is the brand of the subject (Nvidia green `#76b900`-ish, the red of the offer). Text is huge (the "NO" is ~35% of the frame width) and enters by dropping in with blur, by fading up, or digit by digit. There are no subtitles.

## Motion graphics

3D-rendered objects (envelope, stool, hands, logo) as cutouts with soft shadows; thin red wireframe hexagons linking two items; a red glow on the eyes for "vision".

## Colour

Solid colour fields per beat: green (Nvidia), deep red `#470908`–`#8a0f0f` (the offer and the tension), black, and light grey. People are always B&W. One accent colour per beat, the brand colour of the thing being talked about. Saturation mean 0.33, much higher than the paper style.

## Sound design

Measured only: −14.3 LUFS, LRA 1.5, no silences. The VO is a fast English read at 3.2 words/s. SFX were not measured; it most likely has hits on the colour flips, so check by ear.

## VFX

Motion blur, depth-of-field blur, colour flips, a negative flash, glow, 3D object drops.

## Do / don't

Do: the number as a 3D object; a brand-colour field per beat; B&W people; huge display type on the key word; hits on colour flips.
Don't: paper textures (that is the other style); more than one accent per beat; small text.

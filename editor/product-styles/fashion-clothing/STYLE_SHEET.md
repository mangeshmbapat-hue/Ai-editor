## Measured (FFmpeg and OpenCV, from the samples)
_Length, aspect, frame rate, cuts, colour of the background and the product, loudness, and when the sound stops._

## Observed (frame by frame, from the samples)
- **Product reveal:** how the product first appears.
- **Motion and camera:** rotation, zoom, push-in, orbit, float, speed, eases.
- **Light and reflections:** shine, highlights, glow, shadows.
- **Background and colours:** the ground, the colour theme, and how it matches the product.
- **Props and elements:** splashes, particles, ingredients, fabric, smoke, roads.
- **Text:** fonts, sizes, colours, how and when it arrives, glow.
- **Transitions:** how one shot becomes the next.
- **Pace:** shot lengths and the build to the hero shot.
- **End card:** the logo, the CTA and how it lands.
- **Sound:** music, SFX on the moves, and the ending.
